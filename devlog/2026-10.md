## 2026-10-04: Closeness in recommendations, earlier asks, tombstones (iOS)

**What changed**
- Recommendation engine reads closeness: deck and question closeness as a minimum, never relaxed, also on the favorites fallback; `group_only` questions never go to one person
- Depth ceiling when a person has no depth set: not close yet light, kind of close light and medium, close all; unknown closeness counts as kind of close
- Deck rules from `deck_filters.json` via `DeckFilterStore.recommendRules`: `never` out everywhere, `birthday` and `quiet` decks lead a person's picks when met, highest `quiet_days` wins; all rule decks excluded from From a deck you like and the deck recommendation
- Birthday window 3 days before through the day (`birthdayWindowDays`)
- Ask queue: `closeness` added in first place; closeness and depth start after 1 send, may go before a pending follow up once each, wait until the next calendar day; Noted stub hands the slot on after 2 seconds with a fresh pick
- Natural Reach records the closeness hint only when tapped
- Hearts read from `SavedQuestionsStore` only; the player's copy back loop removed; `favoritesByDeck` no longer merged from the cloud
- Tombstones: bookmarked decks (`deckAddedAt`, `deckRemoved`), sent rows (`stampedAt`, `removedAt`), people settings (`fieldStamps` per field, a clear counts as a change); people merge field by field, also when folding by name
- All time maps push as a plain merge; saved questions push split into time maps (merge) and `byDeck` (replaced)
- Saved questions deck shows the current library's copy by id, so it follows the app language
- Sent info sheet shows the language note through the shared `spaceSentLanguageNote`
- `person_matching.json`: `localsecrets` out of `interests.travel`, `foundingsolo` out of `interests.startups`

**Key decisions**
- Closeness picks the decks, depth picks the questions; an explicit depth stays the user's word and may relax to any depth, a derived ceiling never does
- Deck rules live in `DeckFilterStore`, `Deck` model untouched (cache and sync unaffected)
- One source for hearts instead of tombstones for a second list; no migration, since an old heart cannot be told from a ghost
- Time maps only grow and timestamps decide, so a device that has not pulled cannot erase a tombstone; replaced the first `mergeFields` version the same day
- People need no tombstones of their own: a person exists through sends, `pruneOrphans` follows the dead rows
- Sent rows stamp only when recreated under an old date (attach, detach, reassign, add person); a fresh send's `sharedAt` already outranks a tombstone
- `groupOnly` and `fieldStamps` optional so stored saved questions and people still decode
- Asking closeness reverses the 18 August exclusion: the share hint was the default more often than the user's word

**Files changed**
- `SuggestedQuestionEngine.swift`, `PersonPromptEngine.swift`, `DeckFilterStore.swift`, `QuestionItem.swift`, `QuestionStore.swift`, `CloudSync.swift`, `FavoritesStore.swift`, `SharedQuestionsStore.swift`, `PeopleStore.swift`
