## 2026-06-14 — iOS: App Preview Video Fixed + v1.2.2 Submitted

### App Preview video fix
- Root cause confirmed: Apple requires a silent AAC stereo 48kHz audio track even for silent previews. Simulator recordings have no audio track at all, which Apple rejects.
- Additional issues in the source video: wrong color transfer (`iec61966-2-1` sRGB instead of `bt709`), variable frame rate (~44fps instead of constant 30fps), wrong dimensions (1206x2622 instead of 886x1920 for the 6.5" slot).
- Fix pipeline: decode source to raw YUV (`ffmpeg -c:v rawvideo`), re-encode with `fps=30,scale=886:1920`, `color_trc bt709`, then add silent track with `anullsrc=r=48000:cl=stereo`, codec `aac`, bitrate `128k`.
- Final command that worked: two-pass ffmpeg (raw intermediate + final encode with `-f lavfi -i anullsrc`).
- For future App Previews: always add silent AAC audio track. Use 886x1920 for 6.5" slot.
