## 2026-07-04 — iOS: Deployment targets lowered to iOS 18.0 / watchOS 11.0, widget switched to static Inter fonts (Bold question)

### Deployment targets (Option A: one clean, consistent floor)
- Old state was inconsistent Xcode auto bumps: the app inherited iOS 18.5, QwavaWidgetExtension was 26.5, the watch app 26.2, the watch widget 26.5.
- New floor set deliberately and consistently: iOS 18.0 for the app and the widget, watchOS 11.0 for the watch app and the watch widget. Test targets aligned to match.
- Rationale: as of June 2026 iOS 26 is about 79 percent of iPhones and iOS 18 about 14 percent, so an 18.0 floor covers the large majority while letting the promoted widget reach iOS 18 users, which the 26.5 widget floor had been silently blocking. Decided against an iOS 17 fallback: small and shrinking share, not worth the extra availability guards and testing.
- Full scan of all 75 app Swift files plus the widget and watch confirmed nothing uses an iOS 26 or watchOS 26 API, so the move was a Build Settings change with no code changes. The only availability checks in the whole codebase are two `if #available(iOS 18.0, *)` blocks in SpaceQuestionDetailView.swift (the zoom transition); at an 18.0 floor they always take the iOS 18 path, so they were left untouched.

### Widget font: root cause and decision
- Symptom: the daily question rendered heavier in the simulator than on the real phone, and even the letter spacing differed between the two.
- First read was the known variable axis issue: the widget used the Inter variable font with the wght axis applied through kCTFontVariationAttribute, which the simulator honors but the device ignores.
- The deeper cause, found on device, was that the widget was not resolving its font at all and was falling back to the system font, which explains both the wrong weight and the wrong spacing. Different letter advance widths were the tell that it was a different font, not just a different weight.
- The fix has two parts that BOTH have to be true for a widget extension: the font files must be members of the QwavaWidgetExtension target (Copy Bundle Resources), and they must be registered in the widget's OWN Info.plist under UIAppFonts. The widget Info.plist had been missing the registration, so `UIFont(name:)` failed on device and fell back.
- Switched QwavaWidget.swift from the variable axis helper to a plain static face lookup by name. Added Inter-Regular.ttf, Inter-SemiBold.ttf, and Inter-Bold.ttf to the widget target and registered all three in QwavaWidget Info.plist.
- Decision: the widget question text is Inter-Bold, chosen for immediate glanceability on a busy home screen. SemiBold (600) was too subtle a step above Regular, especially as white text on the colored card. Inter-SemiBold.ttf stays in the project and registered, kept as an option if we revisit.

### Files changed
- QwavaWidget/QwavaWidget.swift: variable axis helper replaced with a static face lookup; question weight is Inter-Bold, label stays Inter-Regular. Paddings unchanged from Raf's latest.
- QwavaWidget/Info.plist: UIAppFonts now lists Inter-Regular.ttf, Inter-SemiBold.ttf, Inter-Bold.ttf.
- Qwava/Qwava/Fonts/: added static Inter-Regular.ttf, Inter-SemiBold.ttf, Inter-Bold.ttf, all members of the widget target.
- Xcode project: deployment targets set to iOS 18.0 (app, widget) and watchOS 11.0 (watch app, watch widget), test targets aligned.

### Open / follow up
- The app itself still routes all typography through the same variable font wght axis path (BrandFont in Theme.swift, via interVariable in InterVariableSupport.swift), so on device the app headings likely render at the base regular weight instead of their intended weights. Needs the same treatment as the widget: ship static Inter weights, select by name, keep the Dynamic Type scaling. Its own focused pass.
- Lesson for any future custom font in an extension: add the files to that extension's target AND register them in that extension's own Info.plist; do not rely on the variable font weight axis on device.

### Notes
- Not yet resubmitted to the App Store; these changes are committed but a new build has not been shipped. Raf will test the Bold widget across a few days with different daily questions before deciding anything further.

### Share square as the "Send to someone else" control
- New SpaceShareSquare: a square button to the right of the mini deck, side driven by the mini deck height (aspectRatio 1 plus fixedSize(horizontal: true)), deck background and outline colors, same 24 corner, a white square.and.arrow.up glyph (stays white in dark mode since deck colors are fixed). 4px gap via HStack(spacing: 4). - Removing the text lifted the gap between the question and the bottom row to 32 on the big card and 24 on the small card. The glyph is nudged up with offset(y: -1) since an SF Symbol placed by font metrics reads slightly low.

### Shadow transition lag on minimize
- Symptom: the question card shadow appeared about a second after closing the full screen question with the pull down dismiss. Root cause: the shadow sat inside the view marked as the zoom transition source (.zoomSource), so SwiftUI suppressed it until the interactive dismiss settled. The drawing technique was never the cause; the location was.
- Fix: added a castsShadow flag (default true) to both question card structs. The Your Space, daily, and sent call sites pass castsShadow: false and apply .spaceCardShadow() after .zoomSource(...), outside the source, so it paints immediately. Deck cards and placeholders keep the internal shadow via the default.
- Made spaceCardShadow() internal (was a fileprivate extension) so SpaceSentView and SpaceDailyArchiveView can call it.
- SpaceSentView then hit "compiler unable to type check in reasonable time"; split its two cards into bigCard and smallCard ViewBuilder helpers to lift them out of body.
- Reworked the shadow shape into a short blurred bar pinned to the bottom (height 24, blur 8, pinned bottom, offset y 10) instead of a full card sized rectangle behind the card. The full size rectangle only stayed clean at the top while hosted inside the card; applied outside the source its bounds no longer hid behind the card, so the blur leaked on top and sides. The bottom bar cannot leak upward regardless of host.

### Decks list (SpaceDecksView)
- Fixed the "In deck order" sort: deck numbers are stored as "#01" through "#50", so Int(number) was nil and every deck tied at .max. Now Int(number filtered to digits only), so the digits drive the order.
- Most played sort grouped into five buckets by play count: On repeat (10+), Often (6 to 9), A few times (3 to 5), A couple of times (2), Just once (1). The two lightest buckets render as the compact SpaceHorizontalDeckCard; the rest stay big. Empty buckets hide, matching the recency grouping. Made SpaceHorizontalDeckCard internal so the decks page can use it.
- Recently played sort: split the single "This week" into Today, Yesterday, and Some days ago (Calendar isDateInToday and isDateInYesterday), keeping Last week, "2 to 4 weeks ago", and "Over a month ago".

### App page headers: system font to Inter
- Discovery and correction of an earlier note: the large page titles and their subtitles across the app were rendering in San Francisco, not Inter, because they used SwiftUI system text styles (.largeTitle.weight(.light) and .subheadline) rather than BrandFont. The app's own Inter path does render distinct weights on Raf's device, so the earlier worry that app headings render at a base weight did not hold; the real cause of non Inter headers was these system text styles. Every .font(.system(size:)) usage is on an SF Symbol and correctly stays system.
- Swapped .largeTitle.weight(.light) to BrandFont.h1(weight: .light) (both 34pt) and .subheadline to BrandFont.callout(weight: .regular) across MainTabsView, MixView, FavoritesView, MyQwavaView, MoreView, QuestionsView, and SpaceDetailScaffold. NaturalReachView "Share" title to BrandFont.h1(weight: .regular). SkippedSummaryVie: .title3.semibold to headline.semibold, .body to body, .callout to callout. CreateView .title2 to h3.
- Not touched: nav bar titles set via .navigationTitle (UIKit appearance, mostly empty or hidden), and Text views with no explicit font that fall back to system. Flagged as a possible later sweep.

### Your Space typography and spacing, carousel clip
- Greeting: experimented with size and weight for the time greeting and the name; both on h1 for now, Raf still tuning. Section headers on h3. Several top paddings adjusted by Raf.

## 2026-07-05 (Development 12): Your Space placeholder question cards, lighter placeholder shadows, suggested question section, still to explore carousel and page, deck card sizing pass

### Placeholder question cards get the mini deck and share square
- The placeholder branches of SpaceBigQuestionCard and SpaceSmallQuestionCard still showed the old "from DeckName" text plus a dimmed "Send to someone else?" text, left over from before the July 4 rework. They now render the same mini deck card plus square share button as the real cards, but inert.
- SpaceDeckMiniCard and SpaceShareSquare each gained an isPlaceholder flag. In placeholder mode the fill becomes PlaceholderDeckBg, the outline PlaceholderInk, and on the share square the glyph becomes PlaceholderInk instead of white, so the row reads greyed and inert. The placeholder path does not wrap the mini deck or the share square in buttons, so nothing is tappable.
- SpacePlaceholders.swift: QuestionPlaceholder gained fromSubtitle and imageName, and a new questionDeckCard(_:) helper builds a placeholder PromoCard for the mini deck (title is the fake deck name, subtitle a short tagline, cover one of placeholder_qwava_01 to 03, all text in neutralText). SpaceShareSlot gained a deckCard field so the sent placeholder slots carry their mini deck card.
- Placeholders keep the internal shadow (castsShadow default true), since they are never a zoom source and never had the lag that pushed the real cards to an external shadow.

### Lighter shadow for placeholder cards
- On the muted placeholder tones the shared 0.15 shadow read heavy. spaceCardShadow gained an opacity parameter defaulting to 0.15, and every placeholder capable card (the three deck cards and the two question cards) now passes opacity: isPlaceholder ? 0.05 : 0.15. Real cards keep 0.15 through the default, so only placeholders lightened.

### Suggested question section, "From a deck you like"
- New file SuggestedQuestionEngine.swift (Data/, Qwava target). Pure logic mirroring RecommendationEngine. Picks one question to send from a deck the user likes: favorited decks first (rotated per day for variety), then most played, taking the first deck that still has a question not in the recent sent history. Day stable pick. Returns a Suggestion { deck, question } or nil.
- MyQwavaView: a suggestedQuestion computed property feeds a new "From a deck you like" section placed right after Sent out lately, rendered as a SpaceSmallQuestionCard with the deck's real mini deck and share square, tap to expand full screen, share into Natural Reach. suggestionShare(_:) builds the SharedQuestion payload. Because sending records it, the question then appears in Sent out lately and is excluded from the suggestion next time, so the loop closes cleanly. The section hides when nil (no favorites and no plays).
- Decision: framed honestly as "from a deck you like", not a personalized pick. The person trigger idea (a prompt to think of who to send it to) and a discovery "worth a try" variant are parked for later, layered on top of this.

### Still to explore carousel and see more page
- New section at the bottom of Your Space: a horizontal row of up to six unplayed decks (never crossed a qualified play), mirroring Where you left off. Gated to appear only once at least one deck has been played and something is still unplayed. Day stable shuffle via a small FNV 1a hash seeded by the day, so the six rotate across days but hold within a day, cycling the whole catalog over time.
- MyQwavaView helpers: unplayedDecks, exploreStripDecks (capped 6), daySeed, exploreHash.
- SpaceDecksView: DeckSort gained a stillToExplore case (page title and menu label "Still to explore"), an unplayedDecks property (deck number order), and a content branch that renders the unplayed list with its own empty state ("You have played every deck. Nice."). The carousel See more opens the decks page on this sort. Note it is a different set (the unplayed decks) rather than a reorder of the played set, and the branch runs before the played empty gate.
- Decision: strictly never played, kept separate from the "not played in a while" revisit story, which stays with the Home labels and the recommendation revisit rule. Good future hook for promoting new decks, which are unplayed for everyone.

- Mini deck vertical padding moved from 12 to 10, parameterized as verticalPadding on SpaceDeckMiniCard (default 10). The full screen detail view passes verticalPadding: 12 to keep its larger mini deck unchanged.

New MiniDeckRow wraps the mini deck and the share square, measures the mini deck's rendered height with a GeometryReader and a preference key (MiniDeckHeightKey), and pins the square to that on both sides. All four question card call sites (big and small, real and placeholder) route through MiniDeckRow. The share glyph nudge moved from -1 to -2 for the now correct, slightly larger square.

- Horizontal deck card made self sizing to its text. Removed the fixed height (which had briefly gone from 136 to 120, then to 158 while chasing the number) and the trailing Spacer, and switched to the mini deck fill pattern (Color.clear at width 154, maxHeight infinity, image as an overlay) with fixedSize vertical, so the card height is top 16, title, subtitle, 8 gap, deck number, bottom 16, landing around 122 to 128 and staying correct under Dynamic Type. This component is shared by On heavy rotation, the recommended deck, and the most played buckets on the decks page, so all pick up the tighter self sizing card with no call site changes.
- Vertical deck card made more vertical: the cover turned portrait (was 158 by 154, now 150 by 180 after width tuning), the card width went 166 to 158, the subtitle now allows two lines with reservesSpace so the carousel rows stay even, and the subtitle to number gap rose to 12.
