## 2026-08-02: Person settings, share chips with prefill, people carousel, sign in keep or leave question (iOS)

### What changed
- `PeopleStore.swift`: `Person` gains optional `closeness`, `rhythm`, `depthPreference` (explicit, set only on the settings sheet) and `sharedCloseness` (derived hint from shares); setters, `effectiveCloseness` (explicit first, hint second), `pruneOrphans()`; one orphan sweep per launch, deferred a cycle.
- `SpacePersonSettingsView.swift`: new About section with three segmented pickers (closeness, rhythm, depth), unselected by default, save on tap, per row Clear with accessibility labels; spacing matched to Natural Reach and MoreView rhythm.
- Rhythm keys renamed before first release: `every_week`, `every_month`, `now_and_then`, so keys match the shipped labels with no migration ever needed.
- `NaturalReachView.swift`: person chip row (recent six) above the pickers; tapping prefills closeness from the person and recency derived from send timestamps (ongoing within 14 days, few weeks within 90); sharing with a chip selected attaches the person silently, skips the who prompt, bumps recency, writes the share closeness back as hint; new `preselectedPersonID` input for a later person page entry.
- `SpaceSentInfoSheet.swift`: a send without a person now renders ("No name yet") with Delete from history in its menu; Sent section shows whenever any send exists, so unnamed sends are never stuck.
- `SharedQuestionsStore.swift`: `detachPerson`, `removeShare`, `removeShares` end with `pruneOrphans()`, so a person with zero sends disappears from chips, prompts and sheets immediately.
- `CloudSync.swift`: sign in refactored around a three case rule; keep or leave question fires only when the account has content AND the device holds activity from another owner; new `localDataOwner` marker tells account residue apart from guest activity, so relaunches stay silent; sign out now pushes and clears all five stores (was play stats only); `applyRemote`, `pushAll`, `clearAllLocal` helpers; orphan prune after every pull.
- `RootView.swift`: keep or leave alert; Keep bold as default, no destructive role (which had auto inserted a dead Cancel).
- `MyQwavaView.swift`: Your people chip carousel below the deck suggestion, shown from two people, question card background plus `spaceCardShadow`, navigates to the person pages; first direct door to them.
- Copy passes applied from the Copy chat: person settings About section, Natural Reach chip row and subtitle, merge alert.

### Key decisions
- Explicit person settings are the user's word: shares update the derived hint only, never the sheet values.
- Rhythm (pattern) never feeds the recency prefill (moment); recency reads only from real send timestamps.
- Orphan rule: no sends, no person, settings included; a person exists in Qwava through the questions sent to them.
- Merge question only in the genuinely ambiguous case; guest to first account migration stays silent and untouched.
- Follow ups feature scoped in discussion: per deck plus universal plus later tiers in one `follow_ups.json`, sent follow ups recorded as sends, shared direct without the Natural Reach panel; content being written in the Decks chat.

## 2026-08-04: Search question zoom transition, people chip circles, daily question subline (iOS)

- `HomeSearchResults.swift`: `HomeSearchResultsList` takes a `zoomNS`; question results carry `.zoomSource(id: item.id, in: zoomNS)`, so a result grows open and drags down to close.
- `SpaceCompactCards.swift`: `SpaceCompactQuestionCard` gains `castsShadow` (default true); search passes false and applies `.spaceCardShadow(opacity: 0.12)` outside the source.
- `MyQwavaView.swift`: new `SpacePersonChip` and `SpacePersonChipHeightKey`; circle sized from the measured chip height less 8, filled with the deck's `cardBackgroundColor`, 1pt `strokeBorder` in `cardOutlineColor`, initial in `titleColor`.
- `MyQwavaView.swift`: new `lastSentDeckCard(for:)`; `shares` is stored newest first, so the first match is the latest send.
- `MyQwavaView.swift`: Today's question header split into a `VStack` at spacing 4, subline "The same for everyone, every day." in callout `SublineInk`, `See all` moved onto the subline row.
- `MyQwavaView.swift`: Your people top padding 32 to 40, matching Still to explore; placeholder title comment removed.
- `SpaceSentInfoSheet.swift`: `"No name yet"` to `"No name"`, `"Touch and hold a name for options."` to `"Touch and hold for options."`; header comment rewritten, dropping the stale placeholder note and the outdated Sent section rule.

### Key decisions
- Shadow lag on dismiss is a location problem, not a drawing one; same fix as 4 July, now applied to the compact card because it became a zoom source.
- Initial takes the deck's `titleColor` rather than a hardcoded white; all 50 decks are `#ffffff` today, so it survives a deck changing.
- Circle measured from the chip height, safe because it is an overlay and takes no part in layout, so it cannot drive the height it reads.
- Subline at callout rather than `See all`'s body size: 17pt wraps on every phone, and callout matches the existing `SheetSectionHeader` pattern.
- `lastTextBaseline` on the subline row so `See all` follows the sentence when it wraps at large Dynamic Type.
- `"No names on this one yet."` kept as the empty state: "yet" points forward at the field below it, the helpful direction, where on a row label it pointed backward at something undone.

## 2026-08-07: Follow up layer, people merge fix, person page polish (iOS)

### What changed
- New `FollowUpsStore.swift` (Data): `FollowUpItem` model, bundle load plus CDN refresh with disk cache, tier selection, per person offer state in UserDefaults (`FollowUpOffers.v1`), cleared on `.qwavaResetAll`.
- New `SpaceFollowUpCard.swift` (Screens/MyQwava): offer card at the top of the person page; editable `TextEditor`, `Try another` cycles tiers until edited, `Share` opens its own `FollowUpShareSheet` and records the send on completion; `No thanks` marks the offer handled.
- `RemoteContent.swift`: optional `follow_ups_url` manifest key, `loadFollowUpsData(from:)` with fixed CDN path fallback and cache.
- `QwavaApp.swift`: follow ups bundle load at launch, remote refresh alongside questions.
- `SpaceSentView.swift`: card inserted above the groups; page dismisses itself via `personMissing` watcher when its person is removed; page wide tap resigns the keyboard.
- `SpaceDetailScaffold.swift`: `.scrollDismissesKeyboard(.interactively)` on the scroll view.
- `SpaceSentStub.swift`: `spaceCardShadow()` applied after the context menu; header comment updated to reviewed.
- `PeopleStore.swift`: `mergeRemote` folds same name people into the most recently used, reassigning sends via `reassignPerson(from:to:)`.
- `NaturalReachView.swift`: `shareAppName(for:)` made internal for reuse; `ShareSheet` stays private after a redeclaration clash with `QuestionPlayerView`.
- Copy applied from review: subline "A follow up to the last question you sent.", `Suggested`, `Try another`, `Share`, `No thanks`; title "Keep it going?" approved as drafted.

### Key decisions
- Default tier from recency: question specific under 14 days, later tier beyond, matching the chip threshold; no offer past 30 days so the card never outlives the page window.
- Cycle first, edit last: an edit hides `Try another` rather than risking a silent overwrite.
- One card per person, not per sent question, so the offer stays an offer instead of wallpaper.
- No offer on a follow up (`fu_` prefix guard), stopping the chain cleanly.
- Share sheet over a WhatsApp deep link: a deep link has no completion callback, and honest recording beats one saved tap.
- Not now dismisses silently for the current send with no reminder; reminders belong to the nudge, still parked.
- Keyboard: page tap, card tap and drag dismiss; keyboard bar `Done` built then removed, the floating capsule reads as a stray button.
- Recency prefill thresholds (14 and 90) blessed as final; the 30 day offer window blessed with them.
- Sync duplicate people are a merge bug, not a UX gap: names identify people everywhere else, so `mergeRemote` enforces it too; survivor keeps settings, absorbed settings accepted as lost.
- Stub shadow applied outside the context menu so the lifted preview stays clean.
